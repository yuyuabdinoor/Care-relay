THE SAFETY BOUNDARY

The model cannot give itself permission—or declare itself finished.

Strands agents decide what work to attempt. Deterministic code validates dates, permissions, evidence, causal state, and final readiness. Care Relay coordinates administrative care; it does not diagnose or recommend treatment.
